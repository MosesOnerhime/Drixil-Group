# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Moses confirmed a balanced Group homepage serving partners and clients, with a game-first Interactive page serving players. Visitors need to understand the company, find the appropriate division, explore current games, and reach the relevant contact or community destination.

## Product Purpose

The public Drixil Group website explains the parent company and its focused divisions. Each division has its own route. The website presents real projects and capabilities, distinguishes current work from future plans, and provides clear paths to games, communities, partners, and contact.

## Positioning

Drixil Interactive is an Africa-based game studio building for a global audience. Its goal is original games that meet international standards in gameplay, presentation, and performance. Africa is its base, not a restriction on its audience or subject matter. Beyond 90 is the current focus; Volleyball Rally is also in development. Roblox is the starting platform; PC, mobile, console, and publishing are longer-term ambitions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing implementation: React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind, static prerendering, and the existing Vercel analytics integrations. Retain the working stack.
- Preserve the Group, Interactive, Studio, Labs, Originals, Store, Ventures, and custom 404 routes, useful section anchors, and working destinations.
- Roblox game links must open the actual game pages. Community destinations remain separate actions.
- Interactive uses shared Drixil Interactive social accounts. Preserve the Beyond 90 community links and the confirmed Novatrax partnership.
- Studio includes motion design for product showcases and launches. Portfolio content has not yet been provided. Other division pages must communicate their actual development status.
- Keep the current implementation recoverable through the verified GitHub backup branch. Do not overwrite unrelated instruction-file changes.

## Brand Commitments

Keep Drixil's name, supplied monogram and wordmark, existing brand assets, and accurate company content. Moses requested layouts and concepts similar to Rockstar Games, adapted to the broader Group and its Interactive division rather than copying another company's identity or artwork.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing Drixil logos, Beyond 90 icon and promotional art, Volleyball Rally icon and promotional art, Novatrax logo and official destination, confirmed Roblox game/community links, shared social accounts, current company copy, and the documented company-strategy audit. No invented awards, reviews, client work, launch dates, staff, metrics, legal policies, or additional game announcements.

## Product Principles

- Explain the company before asking a visitor to decode the brand.
- Let real projects demonstrate capability.
- Separate current development, goals, and future expansion.
- Make games and community destinations easy to distinguish.
- Preserve essential information and working behavior across devices and without client-side JavaScript.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Keep keyboard access, visible focus, semantic headings, meaningful image alternatives, readable contrast, generous touch targets, reduced-motion behavior, and equivalent essential content on mobile and desktop. Search readiness must support discovery without promising rankings.
